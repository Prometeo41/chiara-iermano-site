Fashion and event producer working across editorial, advertising and live events. Based in Naples, available for projects throughout Italy.

She turns a creative idea into a finished project: she coordinates editorials and campaigns for brands and magazines, handles casting, location scouting and on-set logistics, and manages the timeline from concept to delivery.

Her work spans fashion editorials, brand campaigns and events that bring together fashion, image and attention to detail — presentations, shows, press days and tailor-made occasions.

She collaborates with photographers, stylists, creative teams and magazines, including Vanity Teen, Man In Town, MMScene and Lewis Magazine.
